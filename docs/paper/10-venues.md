Status: shortlist for the supervisor, checked on 2026-11-11. A date is called **verified**
only when it was read on the conference's or publisher's own page on that day. Dates of
2027 editions that are not announced yet are given as the last edition's pattern and
tagged `[EST]`; they must be re-checked in January–February 2027.

## 1. What the timeline allows `[ASSUMPTION]`

| Milestone | Earliest date | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| Outdoor logging of temperature and irradiance (pilot, load off) | December 2026 | stand, DAQ bring-up |
| Load built and bench-tested; sweeps and γ runs start | January 2027 | load parts arriving in November–December |
| ≥ 50 + 20 labelled events (≈ 20 test days) | March 2027 | weather, team time |
| Analysis and a full draft with real results | April 2027 | the above |

So a venue with a deadline before March 2027 can only take a paper on the instrument,
the method and pilot data. A venue with a deadline from April 2027 onward can take the
full paper.

## 4. Recommendation `[REC]` — the supervisor decides

1. **Plan for the full paper in April–May 2027**, and choose between a conference whose
   2027 call fits that date (GPECOM-type, to re-check in January) and the university's
   own journal, which has no deadline. A journal submission does not depend on travel
   money or on a call that has not been published.
2. **Keep one autumn-2027 conference as the second target** for the same or an extended
   paper: SASG 2027 if it publishes in IEEE Xplore and the dates fit, otherwise ISGT
   Middle East 2027 or ICRERA 2027.
3. **PVSC 55 only if the supervisor wants the stretch**: an abstract by 15 January 2027
   built on the instrument, the uncertainty budget, the method and whatever pilot data
   exists. It is the right audience, but a weak abstract costs a rejection and the trip
   is costly.
4. Do not submit the same results to two venues at once. A conference paper may later
   be extended into a journal article if the journal's rules allow it and the overlap is
   declared.

## 5. Questions for the supervisor

1. Journal or conference first? Is there a budget for registration and travel?
2. Is IEEE Xplore indexing a requirement, or is Scopus indexing enough?
3. When must the paper be *submitted* for it to count for the graduation project?
4. Does he have a preferred Saudi or GCC venue that is not on this list?
